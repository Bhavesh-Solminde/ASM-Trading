What the research says this kind of platform needs

Dark-first is a usability call, not a style

Traders keep the screen open for hours, often at night. Dark surfaces cut glare, but they need stronger contrast discipline: slightly heavier text and chart gridlines at around 6–8% opacity.

Keep green = up and red = down

Changing that convention breaks trust. About 1 in 12 men can't tell red from green, though, so every demo also uses ▲/▼ shapes, +/− signs, status words, a lightness difference between the two colours, and (A, C) hollow up-candles against filled down-candles.

Numbers must not jump

Live prices and countdowns use tabular figures so digits keep the same width. Only values the trader actually watches animate on change.

Order placement is the highest-risk moment

The ticket shows asset, payout, time, stake and "if correct" profit before the button. On Live, a strip says real balance is at stake, and each trade shows its entry line and expiry on the chart.

Retail wants focus, not a terminal

Zerodha Kite, India's reference broker, won on a minimal, fast interface and only later added optional multi-panel layouts. Retail platforms do best with a clear hierarchy: price big, everything else quieter.

Payment trust comes from the waiting state

On a UPI payment, silence is what scares people. Because ASM approves deposits by hand, each demo turns the wait into numbered steps, an "exact amount" warning, a live Processing timer, and plain words for Approved and Rejected.

No dark patterns

Regulators treat these products as high-risk and target bonus-style incentives. The Quotex "50% bonus" promo banner is removed in all three demos, and nothing claims speed, payouts or regulation.

Live vs Demo must be impossible to miss

Quotex only shows it in small text. Each demo gives Live a distinct plate and styling, and puts a DEMO watermark on the chart.

Kept identical across A, B and C

  • Quotex structure: icon rail, asset tabs, centre chart with sentiment bar, right-hand ticket, open/closed trades, tabbed payments page.
  • Four unavailable destinations stay visible but disabled, as the product requires.
  • The real deposit rules from the codebase: $10–$961, the ₹107.64 rate, a unique paise amount per deposit, a 12-digit UTR, and manual approval.
  • Desktop-first at 1440px, adapted to phone width with a bottom tab bar.
