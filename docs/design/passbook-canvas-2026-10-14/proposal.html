Passbook: a redesign for the IndianxTrade app

The app today works but looks like a template: plain blue buttons, empty black login and lock screens, nothing that says "this is safe for my money". This proposal keeps the logo and the dark ground, keeps the chart in charge of the Trade screen, and borrows its trust from the things every Indian already trusts with money: the bank passbook, the cheque and the UPI receipt.

The system

One accent (a deeper version of today's blue), green and red only for Up and Down, gold only for bonus. Words in Anek, a typeface made in India for Latin and Devanagari together, so Hindi screens look designed rather than substituted. Every rupee figure is set in a monospaced face, the way a passbook printer prints entries, so columns of money line up.

Five signature details

  • Amounts in wordsLike a cheque. Stake, deposit and withdrawal amounts are spelled out, in Hindi too, so ₹10,000 can never be mistaken for ₹1,000.
  • One box per digitPIN entry uses the boxes from every Indian bank form.
  • Passbook ledgerHistory has Debit, Credit and Balance columns with a running balance.
  • Rubber stampsWon, Lost, Credited and Processing are stamped, never just coloured.
  • ReceiptsA finished deposit or trade gets a perforated UPI-style receipt.

Other directions I weighed

Passbook was built because it carries trust best. One strong alternative, and the ideas it borrowed from directions that lost.

Scoreboard alternative

Styled after the live cricket score bar on TV: the market as the batting side, the trade countdown as the ball clock, the Up/Down sentiment as a win-predictor bar, results flashing like a boundary. Instantly familiar and very fast to read.

Risk: it makes trading feel like a game. That works against "trust me with real money", which was your main complaint.

  • Airport gate boardKept: a balance that changes stays highlighted until you've seen it.
  • Railway split-flap boardKept: when the balance updates, its digits roll into place.
  • Orienteering mapKept: your trade lines sit on the chart in one reserved colour and never restyle the candles.
  • Dense Japanese web layoutKept: hairline-ruled modules for the long Account list.
